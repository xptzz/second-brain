## 如果你觉得你的生活很无聊
你可以去混一混创投圈
很多人觉得混创投圈很难
其实一点都不难
你只需要呆在世界一线城市
打开luma
多参加几个本地带有vc pitch/ hackathon/builder/ AI 的活动
注册Linkedin 加入所有founder’s group 和学校创业club 
每次进门前就默念今天我要加满在场所有人的联系方式
散场的时候问一下大家要不要去打德扑/掼蛋
不超过一个月你肯定知道创投圈是什么样了
你会认识到你这辈子都认识不完的神人
威力相当于往微信灌了800个俞浩

**翻译：** If you feel like your life is boring,
you can try hanging out in the venture capital scene.
Many people think it’s hard to break into the VC world,
but it’s actually not hard at all.
All you need to do is live in a major global city,
open Luma,
and attend a few local events featuring VC pitches, hackathons, builders, or AI.
Sign up for LinkedIn and join every founder’s group and school startup club.
Before walking in, silently tell yourself, “Today, I’m going to get everyone’s contact info here.”
When the event wraps up, ask everyone if they want to go play Texas Hold’em or Dian Dan.
In less than a month, you’ll definitely know what the VC scene is all about.
You’ll meet so many amazing people that you’ll never be able to get to know them all in a lifetime.
It’s like adding 800 Yu Haos to your WeChat contacts.
